Date: 20 October 2026. This continues the historical 19 October verification record.

## Release Scope

- Public full-book and chapter/section reading, independent of sign-in availability. Original source text, UTF-16 offsets and hashes are preserved; there are no source copy/download controls or DRM claims.
- Numbered Romanian/English chapter labels such as `Capitolul 1` without changing original headings.

- Research improvement proposal in Markdown and a visually verified nine-page PDF. It is a dated proposal, not a claim that proposed research benchmarks are implemented or achieved.
